Chapter 1 The Riverfront Bookstalls

    The morning mist hung low above the river valley as the bell of the old watchtower struck six. Along the stone embankment, bookstalls were opening their wooden shutters one by one, laying out cloth-bound octavos whose cream paper caught the pale autumn sun. Mr. Laurence paused beside Dr. Ward at No. 14 St. James Square to examine a 19.5 cm edition of Dickens.
